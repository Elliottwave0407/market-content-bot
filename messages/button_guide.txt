How to use the buttons on FinGod cards

Every card the bot posts has three buttons under it:

📤 Post to X - opens the X app with the post text already filled in. Save the card image from Telegram and attach it, then post it.

✅ Posted - tap it after you have posted the card on X. The card then shows "Posted on X by <your name> - time" and the buttons disappear for everyone.

⏭ Skip - tap it if the card is not worth posting. The card is marked "Skipped by <your name>" so nobody else spends time on it.

Why we made them: all three of us post from the same FinGod X account. Without a signal, two of us could post the same card, or a good card could be missed. Before posting, check the card: if it already says Posted or Skipped, leave it. If the buttons are still there, it is free to take.

Please remember:
- Tap Posted AFTER you post, not before.
- The bot cannot see X, so the status is only as accurate as our taps.
- A tap cannot be undone, so tap carefully.
