About Shilpa Medicare Limited (SML)
Shilpa Medicare Limited is a research-driven and vertically integrated pharmaceutical company,
headquartered in Raichur, India. Guided by its purpose of Bringing Complex Molecules to Medicines, the
Company combines scientific and manufacturing expertise across high-potency oncology APIs, Peptides,
Complex Injectables, Novel Drug Delivery Systems, Biologics, NBEs and CDMO services. Its manufacturing
facilities are approved by international regulatory authorities, and it serves partners and patients across the
United States, Europe, Japan, MENA region, and other large international markets. Shilpa Medicare is
committed to making high-quality medicines more accessible worldwide.
